# Brasalands
 I chose Brasalands because for the past 6 years I have been working in a barbershop, while this is a resturant. We have similar problems we work with suppliers for products and how we can manage each one and test their qualities. We also Manage way too many people since it takes ~20mins per haircut and sometimes it gets hard tracking which person came in first and who came in after. One milestone I am very much looking forward to is an app that recommends based on locations you have visted, say you haven't been at this resturant but you have eaten similar food, it will rank and determine which is better and maybe learn more about your tastebuds.
# MY AGENT IDEA for the technology at Brasalands
 The agent would automatically collect sales and customer data from each Brasaland location. It would analyze which menu items are selling well or poorly at each location and identify differences in customer preferences between countries and individual restaurants. If an item consistently performs poorly at a specific location, the system could recommend removing it from that location's menu rather than removing it across the entire chain. The agent could also learn from customer purchase history and use those preferences to recommend menu items and help automate personalized marketing campaigns.
