# Brasaland

I chose Brasaland because for the past 6 years I have been working in a barbershop, while this is a restaurant. We have similar problems because we work with suppliers for products and need to manage each one and test their quality. We also manage many people, and since it takes around 20 minutes per haircut, sometimes it gets difficult to track which person came in first and who came in afterward.

One milestone I am very much looking forward to is an app that recommends restaurants and food based on locations you have visited and your previous purchases. For example, if you haven't been to a particular restaurant but have eaten similar food elsewhere, the app could use that information to recommend relevant restaurants and learn more about your food preferences.

## My Agent Idea for the Technology at Brasaland

The agent would automatically collect sales and customer data from each Brasaland location. It would analyze which menu items are selling well or poorly at each location and identify differences in customer preferences between countries and individual restaurants. If an item consistently performs poorly at a specific location, the system could recommend removing it from that location's menu rather than removing it across the entire chain.

The agent could also learn from customer purchase history and use those preferences to recommend menu items and help automate personalized marketing campaigns.
